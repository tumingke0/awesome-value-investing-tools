## Interactive Simulators

Tools that let you practice judgment, not just calculation.

- [Warren Buffett Life Simulator](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/buffett-simulator.html) - Face 7 pivotal decisions from Buffett's 60-year career — blind — before seeing what he actually chose and why. Zero login.
- [Value Investor Mental Model Quiz](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/investor-bias-check.html) - Test your cognitive biases as an investor. Anchoring, loss aversion, recency bias — find yours before the market does.

## Screeners & Calculators

- [10x Stock Screener](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/10x-stock-screener.html) - Filter stocks by moat strength, management quality, and margin of safety. Free, browser-based, no sign-up.
- [Value Investing Calculator](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/value-investing-calculator.html) - Buffett-style DCF intrinsic value estimator. Input your own assumptions. Free.
- [Investment Punch Card](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/investment-punch-card.html) - Track a lifetime of investment decisions with Munger's 20-punch-card constraint. Forces deliberate thinking before every buy.
- [Sell Signal Checklist](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/sell-signal-checklist.html) - Structured decision framework for when (and whether) to sell a position.
- [Finviz](https://finviz.com/) - Stock screener with fundamental and technical filters. Free tier is substantial.
- [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net/) - 20+ years of financial data for US-listed companies. Free, no login.
- [Simply Wall St (Free)](https://simplywall.st/) - Visual stock analysis with snowflake chart. Limited free tier.

## Research & Data

- [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar) - Primary source for 10-K, 10-Q, proxy filings. Free, complete.
- [Wisesheets Free](https://wisesheets.io/) - Pull financial data into Google Sheets with formulas. Free tier available.
- [Stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/) - Clean financial statements, ratios, and segment data. Free for most tickers.
- [Tikr.com (Free)](https://tikr.com/) - 10 years of financial history for international stocks. Free with sign-up.
- [DisconfirmAI Free Tools](https://disconfirmai.com/en/tools) - 23 free research outlines and dashboards for testing a thesis before trusting it. Includes a Value Trap Review research framework for stocks that look cheap, a Quality Gate checklist and a classic DCF outline. The research app also gives 100 free credits a day.
- [Grassroots Research Checklist](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/grassroots-research-checklist.html) - Field-research questions to ask before buying a consumer-facing business. Channel checks, supplier calls, competitor pricing.

## Learning Resources

- [Buffett's Annual Letters (Berkshire)](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html) - 50+ years of investment thinking, free and primary source.
- [Munger's Poor Charlie's Almanack](https://www.poorcharliesalmanack.com/) - Free PDF release of the complete book.
- [Columbia Business School Value Investing](https://www8.gsb.columbia.edu/valueinvesting/) - Syllabi and reading lists from the Graham & Dodd tradition. Free.
- [Aswath Damodaran's Teaching Materials](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/) - Complete valuation course materials, spreadsheets, datasets. All free.
- [CFA Institute Research & Policy](https://www.cfainstitute.org/research) - White papers and research on equity valuation. Partially free.
- [China Stock Toolbox](https://ordinarymantrying.com/tools/chinese-stock-toolbox.html) - Quick-reference of key Chinese stock market concepts, jargon, and frameworks. For investors researching A-share companies.

## Books

Classic texts. Amazon links below are affiliate links — buying through them supports maintaining this list.

- [The Intelligent Investor](https://amzn.to/3XvZDJ9) — Benjamin Graham (1949). The foundation. Still relevant.
- [Security Analysis](https://amzn.to/4bKTe7n) — Graham & Dodd (1934/2008). The technical companion to above.
- [The Snowball: Warren Buffett and the Business of Life](https://amzn.to/4w2cpWB) — Alice Schroeder (2008). Best primary-source biography of Buffett's decision process.
- [Poor Charlie's Almanack](https://amzn.to/3XyUVjb) — Charles Munger. Mental models for investing and life.
- [Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits](https://amzn.to/4cLqfUO) — Philip Fisher (1958). Growth investing through quality-first lens.
- [One Up on Wall Street](https://amzn.to/3XvXXXX) — Peter Lynch (1989). Practical advantage of the ordinary investor.

## Communities

- [r/ValueInvesting](https://www.reddit.com/r/ValueInvesting/) - 300k+ members. Mostly quality, low noise.
- [r/SecurityAnalysis](https://www.reddit.com/r/SecurityAnalysis/) - More rigorous. Original write-ups and thesis discussion.
- [Value Investors Club](https://www.valueinvestorsclub.com/) - Top-tier community. Membership is selective, but past ideas are public after 45 days.
- [Safal Niveshak](https://www.safalniveshak.com/) - Value investing education from an India-based practitioner. Free newsletter.
